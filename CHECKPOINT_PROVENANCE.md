Adapter checkpoints are derived from their corresponding frozen or fine-tuned base checkpoints and are not included. The local experiment archive supplies hashes and selected epochs for the checkpoints used, but does not retain every original training log or source download record. The FFA epoch-50 base lineage, Restormer deraining-weight lineage, and DehazeFormer stopping rationale are owner-confirmed, but their original run evidence is incomplete; do not present these details as independently audited.
